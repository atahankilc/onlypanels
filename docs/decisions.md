| D41 | Address suggestions come from Photon while typing, Nominatim as fallback | Proposed | Nominatim's rules forbid search as you type |
| D42 | One building per request for now. Garages and sheds cannot be added; a drawn garden area still can. The panel's close button or Esc goes back to picking | Confirmed (user) | Simpler flow |
| D43 | All controls come from `web/src/ui.tsx` (Button, IconButton, Segmented, Field, Input, Select, Check, Slider, Section). Two sizes, 36 and 44. Map controls and credits are ours too, built from them | Confirmed (user) | Hand-styled controls drifted apart |
| D44 | Buildings cut by the map's tile grid are put back together by shape in `web/src/stitch.ts`: same height and a real overlap, never by id | Proposed | A building across a tile edge came back as separate pieces, which drew straight lines through blocks and picked half a building. | D45 | Step by step: the product is reduced to its first step, finding and selecting a home, in Munich and surroundings only. Later steps return one at a time. Replaces D42 | Confirmed (user) | Users choose buildings themselves; exact data belongs to the next screen |
| D47 | Selectable buildings come from Bavaria's measured data, not OpenStreetMap, so what is selected is exactly what the next screen shows. OSM blocks remain only as a rough picture when zoomed out. Replaces the tile stitching of D39 and D44 | Confirmed (user) | OSM often draws several houses as one building |
| D50 | Review step: aerial photo on roofs, sunlight per square metre for the year or a chosen time. The user clicks any roof or wall to add or remove it. Walls against a neighbour are left out. Assumed: half of sunlight is diffuse, buildings only, no trees | Confirmed (user) | Users need to see where the sun really falls and keep the final say, with as little UI as possible |
| D51 | Aerial photos use Bavaria's free 20 cm service (CC BY 4.0) instead of 40 cm | Confirmed (user) | Roof photos were too blurry |

| D53 | On the review step the map rises into a window on the right and the panel sits on the dark base. Sunlight shows on every surface. The sun is light on the window, tied to direction only, not an object in the 3D scene | Confirmed (user) | Selection was hard to read, and a 3D sun moved with the map |

| D48 | Buildings load off the page. The API reads each raw square once, in separate processes, and keeps ready-made files in `data/cache/lod2/built-v1/`.
