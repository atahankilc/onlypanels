# Demo script (draft, five minutes)

Follows the brief's four beats. Use held-out projects so step 4 is honest.

| Time | Beat | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Problem | Title | Planners spend hours per design, and the first credible offer often wins |
| 0:30 | 1. A home is described | Planner fills in a few facts, or dictates them | Only what a planner knows between site visits |
| 1:15 | 2. A complete system appears | PV, battery, wallbox and heat pump as appropriate, with reasons, confidence and similar homes | It decides like this installer, using their real catalog |
| 2:15 | 3. The design is refined | "The family is buying an electric car next year". PV, battery and wallbox update, and the changes are explained | The components move together, because the decisions are coupled |
| 3:15 | "As appropriate" | A second home where the right answer is no battery | Not every home gets everything |
| 3:45 | 4. Checked against reality | Our design next to the expert's, then scores across held-out projects and the noise floor | Scored on homes it never saw, compared to how much experts differ from themselves |
| 4:30 | Value | Time per offer, consistency, junior planners | Each planner correction makes it better. More time on roofs |

## Prepare

- Three seeded homes: typical bundle, no battery, oil heating with heat pump
- All three work with no network connection, except the text box
- A recorded fallback, required by Sunday 13:00
