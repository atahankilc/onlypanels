# Demo script (five minutes)

**One idea: data is everything.** Past installer projects hold almost nothing about the home: no roof, no address, no shade. On that data, any model is barely better than giving every home the same design. So the product is a machine for getting the facts: the map measures what the homeowner cannot know, the questions ask only what moves the design, and the planner sees where every fact came from, how wide the honest range is, what it costs under today's rules, and which missing fact would still change the offer.

Numbers below were read off the screen on 8 November with the three seeded requests (`web/src/demo.ts`), all just sent and not yet opened. They come from the engine and the price book and move if either changes (tree shade and prices can shift them a little): walk the script once before recording.

## Optional beat: voice

Planner: the talk ball at the bottom left of the desk. On Sondermeierstraße 74, ask "what does it cost without a battery?". Fits after the money beat; take the 15 seconds from "Facts from open data". Needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`.

## Prepare

- API and web running (`uv run uvicorn main:app --app-dir api --port 8000`, `cd web && pnpm dev`). Restart the API after any engine change.
- The three seeded requests load with the app (`web/src/demo.ts`): Sondermeierstraße 74 (gas, heat pump wanted, electric car, exact bill), Sondermeierstraße 86 (five parts of one complex, oil, use as a range, sauna, low cost first), Am Blütenring 42 (knows little, a car within a year).
- Start clean: forget approved offers (`curl -X DELETE localhost:8000/api/approved`, or "Forget" in a Design tab), or earlier test sends move the drafts. Keep the price book at its defaults (Prices page, nothing marked as your price; `data/cache/pricebook.json` holds the changes).
- Open each seeded request once before recording: designs, shade, prices and PVGIS answers are then cached and appear at once.
- Map tiles, address search and sun data need the network. Squares already visited are cached.
- Without `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` every beat above still works: the review, the money and the rules are fixed rules over the engine. Voice and the photo obstacle check need the key.
- The two slides take their numbers from `README.md` and `docs/data/findings.md` (aggregates only). Past single projects stay out of anything that leaves the team until Q2 is answered.
- A recorded fallback, by 13:00.
